# LabReserve Design Direction

## Identity & Brand
- **Product:** LabReserve (Academic Laboratory Management & Reservation Portal)
- **Purpose:** University system for lecturers and lab officers to schedule, review, and allocate institutional laboratories and research spaces.
- **Character:** Architectural, scholarly, calm, dependable, high-contrast, uncluttered.
- **Timezone:** Asia/Kuala_Lumpur (MYT UTC+8).

## Palette
- **Canvas / Background:** `#f5f8f6` (subtle warm sage tint)
- **Surfaces / Cards:** `#ffffff` (crisp white, 1px border `#e2ebe6`)
- **Primary / Pine:** `#164e43` (deep spruce green, authoritative and tranquil)
- **Primary Hover / Focus:** `#1f6f60`
- **Accent Sage / Tint:** `#eaf4f0`
- **Text Primary:** `#13221e` (high contrast dark spruce, WCAG AAA)
- **Text Secondary / Muted:** `#486057` (exceeds WCAG AA 4.5:1 contrast)
- **Border Subtle:** `#e2ece7`
- **Status Confirmed:** `#1d7350` on `#e8f7f0`
- **Status Pending:** `#99610b` on `#fef5e7`
- **Status Rejected / Inactive:** `#b03838` on `#fdeeed`

## Typography
- **Headings:** `Manrope`, sans-serif (800 / 700 / 600 weight)
- **Body:** `DM Sans`, sans-serif (400 / 500 / 600 weight)
- **Monospace / Identifiers:** Tabular clean mono for booking references (e.g. `LR-2026-X89J`) and lab codes (e.g. `LAB-CS-101`)

## Interaction & Motion Dials
- **Energy:** 1 (quiet, functional academic tool)
- **Rhythm:** 1 (clear hierarchy, grid-aligned, content-first)
- **Motion:** 1 (subtle transitions: 150ms opacity/color transitions, no gratuitous bouncing or loops)
